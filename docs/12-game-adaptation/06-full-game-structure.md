# 06 — Full-Game Structure

The ten screenplay sequences (`10A_sequence_map.csv`) map to ten candidate game
chapters. **Playtime is deliberately uneven** — some sequences are dense
interactive systems, others are best as short playable-plus-cinematic beats. This is
a *candidate* map, not a production commitment; only Chapter 4 (Sequence 04) is
proposed for first release (`07`, `15`).

Legend — **Production complexity:** L / M / H. **First-release suitability:** ✅ yes /
◑ later / ✕ not first.

---

## Chapter 1 — The City Before the Storm (`SEQ-01`, ACT I, 15 min film)

- **Chapter purpose:** Teach *observation* and *hunger-as-visible-inequality*; meet
  the household world; establish that a prosperous city still imprisons people (10A).
- **Playable character:** Manimekalai (with Sudhamathi).
- **Principal location:** Puhar harbour/market/performance quarter (`PD-001`; `SB-001`).
- **Dominant mechanic:** Traversal + Observe (`GM-01`,`GM-02`) — read labour behind wealth.
- **Secondary mechanic:** Listen (`GM-03`) — first testimonies.
- **Emotional movement:** wonder → unease (abundance beside exclusion).
- **Fixed canonical event:** Manimekalai carries inherited identity/grief; the city's plurality and inequality are established.
- **Player agency:** whom to observe/listen to first; what inequality you *notice*.
- **Playtime:** ~35–45 min. **Complexity:** M. **First release:** ◑ (strong tutorial candidate later).

## Chapter 2 — The Woman Who Refuses a Destiny (`SEQ-02`, ACT I, 15 min)

- **Purpose:** Establish **consent and boundary** as the game's spine; desire ≠ permission.
- **Character:** Manimekalai; Sudhamathi's survivor-knowledge as a "read danger" tool.
- **Location:** Flower garden / streets of Puhar.
- **Dominant mechanic:** Dialogue-with-consequence + safe-routing (`GM-06` proto).
- **Secondary:** Observe (reading a public gaze as threat).
- **Emotional movement:** admiration turning to pressure → refusal → forced flight.
- **Fixed event:** refusal of Udayakumaran; goddess-driven removal to Manipallavam.
- **Player agency:** *how* the refusal is spoken and how Sudhamathi reads/reports danger; **not** whether it happens (`RISK-006`).
- **Playtime:** ~25–35 min. **Complexity:** M. **First release:** ✕ (handles consent themes needing full review).

## Chapter 3 — The Island of Memory (`SEQ-03`, ACT I, 15 min)

- **Purpose:** Separate **revelation from complete knowledge**; receive the bowl as *responsibility*.
- **Character:** Manimekalai (isolated); Sudhamathi later gives the human account.
- **Location:** Manipallavam — pond, sacred seat.
- **Dominant mechanic:** Environmental storytelling + Memory (`GM-09` light).
- **Secondary:** Classify (`GM-04`) — "memory" enters the Ledger as its own category.
- **Emotional movement:** fear/isolation → sober responsibility (not triumph).
- **Fixed event:** past-life memory; the Amudhasurabhi comes to her.
- **Player agency:** what fragments she attends to; how she frames memory (never as a present claim, `RISK-007`).
- **Playtime:** ~20–30 min. **Complexity:** M–H (supernatural staging, `11`). **First release:** ✕.

## Chapter 4 — The Bowl and the Hungry City (`SEQ-04`, ACT II, 20 min) — ★ FIRST RELEASE

- **Purpose:** The full core loop: listen → learn hunger-as-system → organise relief →
  reform an institution → reflect. This is the game's thesis in one chapter.
- **Character:** Manimekalai (+ Madhavi, Sudhamathi, Aravana, Aadhirai; embedded Aputhiran).
- **Location:** Aravana's dwelling · Aadhirai's threshold · Ulaga Aravi square · Royal prison.
- **Dominant mechanic:** Amudhasurabhi feeding-logistics (`GM-07`) + institutional reform (`GM-10`).
- **Secondary:** Listen/Classify (`GM-03`/`GM-04`) + embedded playable memory (`GM-09`).
- **Emotional movement:** duty → competence → the relief of "Enough" → sober power-awareness.
- **Fixed event:** Aadhirai's first offering; public feeding; prison → care institution.
- **Player agency:** how relief is organised; who becomes a custodian; whether reform is durable.
- **Playtime:** ~45–60 min. **Complexity:** M (2.5D, no crowds-tech beyond stylised). **First release:** ✅ **(the vertical slice — see `07`).**

## Chapter 5 — The Prince and the Shadow (`SEQ-05`, ACT II, 20 min)

- **Purpose:** The tragedy of **mistaken inference**; the game's hardest epistemics test.
- **Character:** perspective may *shift* to Kanchanan for the mistaken-inference beat (embedded), returning to Manimekalai — to make the player *feel* how certainty is manufactured.
- **Location:** Puhar streets, hidden places.
- **Dominant mechanic:** Investigation/Classify under pressure (`GM-04`,`GM-05`).
- **Secondary:** Trust/witness safety (`GM-06`).
- **Emotional movement:** dread → helpless clarity.
- **Fixed event:** **Udayakumaran's death** by Kanchanan's mistaken inference (`RISK-008`).
- **Player agency:** what the player *classifies* and protects; **never** prevention of the death (`05`).
- **Playtime:** ~30–40 min. **Complexity:** H (perspective shift, sensitive staging). **First release:** ✕.

## Chapter 6 — The Palace of Grief (`SEQ-06`, ACT II, 20 min)

- **Purpose:** Institutional coercion; **nonviolent survival**; grief ≠ guilt.
- **Character:** Rajamadevi (grief) *and* Manimekalai (custody) — dual POV.
- **Location:** Palace, confined room.
- **Dominant mechanic:** Testimony/evidence preservation + endurance (`GM-04`,`GM-06`,`GM-11`).
- **Secondary:** counter-coercion via witnessing (no violence, Pillar 5).
- **Emotional movement:** injustice → refusal of false guilt → naming the harm.
- **Fixed event:** drugging, defamation, starvation-as-coercion; Manimekalai does not accept false guilt (`RISK-009`/`010`).
- **Player agency:** how harm is *named and recorded*; witness safety; **not** escaping the coercion arc.
- **Playtime:** ~30–40 min. **Complexity:** H (trauma safeguards, `GRT-07`). **First release:** ✕.

## Chapter 7 — The Queen Learns to Listen (`SEQ-07`, ACT III, 18 min)

- **Purpose:** Accountability and **relinquishment of control**; testimony convenes.
- **Character:** Manimekalai; Aravana + witnesses (Madhavi, Sudhamathi, Chitrapathi).
- **Location:** Queen's gate/court.
- **Dominant mechanic:** Convening witnesses / testimony assembly (`GM-06` payoff).
- **Secondary:** Dialogue of accountability.
- **Emotional movement:** confrontation → the queen stepping away from the door.
- **Fixed event:** Manimekalai leaves through her own declared path, without royal ownership (`FU-043`).
- **Player agency:** which witnesses are safely present; whether a durable rule is sought (mirrors `VENPA-USE-023`, *proposed only* — game may model the theme, not cite it as approved).
- **Playtime:** ~25–35 min. **Complexity:** M. **First release:** ✕.

## Chapter 8 — The King Who Was a Beggar (`SEQ-08`, ACT III, 15 min)

- **Purpose:** Rebirth memory vs. **present political duty**; governance of hunger.
- **Playable character:** **Punniyarajan** (a rare protagonist swap) — the player governs, not begs.
- **Location:** Javaka court; Manipallavam return.
- **Dominant mechanic:** Light governance/policy choices (storage, water, access) — *management* framed as duty, not city-builder.
- **Secondary:** Embedded Aputhiran continuity (`GM-09`).
- **Emotional movement:** disturbance at an unremembered life → chosen present duty.
- **Fixed event:** Punniyarajan chooses duty over self-erasure (`AD-0142`).
- **Player agency:** governance approach; **not** abandoning subjects for private liberation (Janamithran's warning).
- **Playtime:** ~25–35 min. **Complexity:** M–H (new POV + systems). **First release:** ✕.

## Chapter 9 — The Questions of Truth (`SEQ-09`, ACT III, 17 min)

- **Purpose:** Comparative inquiry; **strongest-form** reconstruction; no winner.
- **Character:** Manimekalai (altered sage form).
- **Location:** Vanji — "a living intellectual city" (`05B`).
- **Dominant mechanic:** Reconstruct-the-argument (steelmanning) + Ledger's *philosophical claim* category; then Aravana's perception/inference/fallacy lesson.
- **Secondary:** Dialogue.
- **Emotional movement:** certainty-seeking → richer questions.
- **Fixed event:** she does not defeat any school; leaves with sharper questions (10A; `PHL-02`–`PHL-05`).
- **Player agency:** encounter order/framing; which strong forms she voices; **no** "correct religion" (`05`; 10A #12).
- **Playtime:** ~30–40 min. **Complexity:** H (writing-heavy, review-heavy `RISK-014`). **First release:** ✕.

## Chapter 10 — The Bowl Beyond Its Owner (`SEQ-10`, ACT III, 10 min)

- **Purpose:** Causation made practical; **the bowl cannot solve a famine — governance
  and continuation must**; disciplined compassionate continuation.
- **Character:** Manimekalai (+ Kanchi communities, king).
- **Location:** Kanchi under famine.
- **Dominant mechanic:** The feeding system at *regional* scale hitting water/crop/
  storage/price/access/language walls — forcing advocacy over service (`GM-07`→`GM-10`).
- **Secondary:** Reflection/epilogue assembly (`GM-12`).
- **Emotional movement:** resolve without closure — commitment, not perfection.
- **Fixed event:** dependent-arising named; refuge; continuation "no single owner can complete".
- **Player agency:** the final aftermath aggregation (`05`); **not** a perfected world.
- **Playtime:** ~20–30 min. **Complexity:** M. **First release:** ✕ (strong *finale*, needs the whole arc).

**Full-game estimate:** ~5.0–6.5 h of the 8–12 h target from these ten chapters;
the remainder is optional embedded memories, side-testimonies, and the reflective/
epilogue layer. **Do not build all ten as the first milestone** (`GRT-01`).

## Sequencing recommendation for production

1. **Chapter 4 first** (vertical slice) — it contains the widest span of core
   mechanics in one bounded space (`07`).
2. Then **Chapters 1 + 3** (they set up Chapter 4 and share traversal/observe/memory
   systems already built).
3. **Chapters 5–7** last among Act II/III — highest cultural/trauma review burden
   (`RISK-006`/`008`/`009`/`010`), so they wait for specialist sign-off.
