## Outcome

The Tamil sequence preserves the governing distinctions:

- prosperity is produced through maintained systems and ordinary labour;
- gratitude may choose a royal face without proving royal causation;
- a ruler may seek knowledge only after accounting for the cost of absence;
- delegation is possible, disappearance is not;
- previous-life continuity does not erase present identity;
- renunciation may create consequences for dependants;
- Aputhiran's ethical inheritance requires Punniyarajan to govern differently, not vanish.

Janamithran and Punniyarajan have distinct performance rhythms: the minister speaks in operational consequences, while the king moves from metaphysical unease toward administrative commitment.
