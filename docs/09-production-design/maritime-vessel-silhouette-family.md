# Maritime Vessel Silhouette Family 0.1

**Control family:** `PD-003`

**Specialist gate:** `SR-010` — maritime archaeology and early Indian Ocean vessel traditions

**Status:** `[INTERPRETATION]` — **NOT AN EXACT ANCIENT SHIP RECONSTRUCTION**

## Purpose

This plate gives storyboard, art, VFX and marine departments a controlled family of functional vessel silhouettes. It prevents one generic “ancient ship” from appearing in every river, harbour, coastal and ocean setting.

The silhouettes express differences in water depth, cargo transfer, voyage range and screen function. They do **not** establish an exact Puhar hull, construction system, sail plan, steering arrangement, timber species or vessel name.

## Confidence scale

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `FC-H` | High confidence that the production needs this function |
| `FC-M` | Moderate confidence in the specific functional distinction |
| `PF-L` | Low confidence in a Puhar-specific complete form |
| `PF-N` | No approved complete form may be reconstructed |
| `CAM-N` | Near or working-detail views allowed after specialist review |
| `CAM-M` | Medium-distance views; avoid unsupported construction detail |
| `CAM-F` | Far silhouette only until the design gate closes |
| `CAM-X` | No intact vessel depiction may be inferred from the evidence |

## Controlled family

### `V1` — River and estuary work craft

- **Function:** Kaveri channels, shallow margins, baskets, grain, people and small working loads.
- **Confidence:** `FC-H / PF-L / CAM-N`.
- **Approved silhouette logic:** shallow working profile, open load area and propulsion suited to constrained water.
- **Unresolved:** exact length, plank system, bow/stern form and whether a sail is present.
- **Screen rule:** labour and water interaction may be shown closely; construction details remain provisional.

### `V2` — Harbour transfer boat

- **Function:** move people and cargo between shore, wharf, estuary anchorage and larger vessels.
- **Confidence:** `FC-H / PF-L / CAM-N`.
- **Approved silhouette logic:** broad working floor, stable loading behaviour and room for multiple paddlers or rowers.
- **Unresolved:** fastening, fenders, covers, steering and exact capacity.
- **Screen rule:** loading choreography may be shown closely; avoid presenting one complete hull as archaeologically recovered.

### `V3` — Coastal merchant craft

- **Function:** staged movement between coastal ports and nearby islands.
- **Confidence:** `FC-M / PF-L / CAM-M`.
- **Approved silhouette logic:** higher sides than `V1`/`V2`, protected cargo and capacity for provisions and repair materials.
- **Unresolved:** sail geometry, mast arrangement, steering system, beaching method and construction tradition.
- **Screen rule:** show as a working vessel at medium distance; rig and hull close-ups require `SR-010` approval.

### `V4` — Ocean-going merchant silhouette

- **Function:** longer Bay of Bengal or connected Indian Ocean voyages.
- **Confidence:** `FC-M / PF-L / CAM-F`.
- **Approved silhouette logic:** greater freeboard and visible capacity for cargo, water, shelter and repair.
- **Unresolved:** the complete form, scale, internal framing, sail plan, steering system, timber and regional type name.
- **Screen rule:** use as a distant or partially obscured working silhouette until specialist review and handling-model tests are complete.

### `V5` — Wreck and debris evidence

- **Function:** represent storm consequence, survivor vulnerability and goods separated from the vessel.
- **Confidence:** `FC-H / PF-N / CAM-X` for intact reconstruction.
- **Approved image logic:** separated timber, rope, cargo, cloth and human survival action may be staged when individually supported.
- **Unresolved:** the intact parent hull.
- **Screen rule:** wreck fragments must never be assembled into a claimed exact vessel merely because the story requires a shipwreck.

## Relationship to the harbour system

The production model is distributed:

1. `V1` works within river and delta channels.
2. `V2` connects shore and wharf with deeper anchorage.
3. `V3` links coastal ports and nearby islands.
4. `V4` remains in deeper water when channel depth is inadequate.
5. `V5` records loss and vulnerability without creating false technical certainty.

This is compatible with a river landing, wharf, estuary anchorage, offshore roadstead and warehouse belt. It is not a claim that every element existed in one fixed arrangement at one date.

## Construction and rig restrictions

Until `SR-010` closes:

- sewn- or stitched-plank construction remains a candidate, not a Puhar-specific fact;
- no hull timber species may be captioned as certain;
- no exact ancient Tamil vessel name may be attached to a silhouette;
- no modern dhow silhouette may serve as an Indian Ocean shortcut;
- no Mediterranean galley or European square-rigged merchantman may be imported;
- no Roman-colonial harbour hierarchy may be implied;
- no medieval Chola naval design may be moved backward in time;
- no modern reconstruction may be copied as an exact ancient original;
- no fantasy galleon scale, decorative rigging or floating-palace treatment is allowed.

## Department use

| Department | Allowed now | Held for review |
|---|---|---|
| Storyboard | water-depth, transfer and scale relationships | close technical hull or rig detail |
| Art department | silhouette alternatives and working zones | final build drawings |
| Marine action | loading, paddling, anchorage and transfer studies | full sailing claims and performance envelope |
| VFX | distant fleet layering and partial occlusion | photoreal hero vessel lock |
| Publicity | “interpretive functional vessel family” | “exact Sangam/Puhar ship” claims |

## Approval path

1. `SR-010` reviews chronology, region, comparative evidence and uncertainty labels.
2. Art and marine departments build low-cost scale/handling models for `V1`–`V4`.
3. Camera tests determine where detail exceeds the approved confidence level.
4. Every proposed hero-vessel detail enters the visual uncertainty register.
5. The family is revised before construction drawings or publicity claims are locked.
