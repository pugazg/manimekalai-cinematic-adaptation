# Food, Vessel and Service Workflow Plate 0.1

## Status and evidence boundary

This plate implements `PD-007`. It coordinates art, props, food styling,
locations, costume, movement, sound and continuity across Aadhirai's household,
Ulaga Aravi, the transformed prison and Kanchi's relief network.

It is `[INTERPRETATION]` grounded in `03F`, `04K`, the approved Aadhirai Venpa
decisions and the bilingual screenplay. It does not certify one ancient menu,
recipe, vessel material, kitchen plan or sanitation code. `SR-002`, `SR-003`,
`SR-007`, `SR-011` and `SR-013` remain open.

## Governing dramatic order

The visual and operational order is fixed:

```text
stored household food
→ human preparation
→ Aadhirai's voluntary gift
→ first portions served
→ unchanged food level discovered
→ crowd and access pressure
→ organised water, serving and cleaning
→ institutional conversion
→ distributed famine relief
```

Magic may continue food portions. It may not prepare water, interpret speech,
identify illness, make a queue safe, wash a vessel, reopen a workshop or correct
an unjust record.

## Workflow zones

| Code | Zone | Minimum visible function | Must not become |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Z1 — arrival` | People approach without being displayed as a devotional crowd | greeting, urgent-need recognition, route choice, seated option | spectacle line proving Manimekalai's holiness |
| `Z2 — drinking water` | Water is offered independently of food | source/container, cup or pour method, refill path, spill control | decorative jar or water implied by food |
| `Z3 — food custody` | Prepared food and Amudhasurabhi are handled safely and accountably | custodian, cover, serving tool, clean working surface | altar, throne or unattended treasure display |
| `Z4 — serving` | Portions reach people at standing and seated heights | measured tool, two-way communication, error correction | magical self-service or modern buffet |
| `Z5 — eating and rest` | People retain time, shade, relationships and choice | seating/rest options, children with carers, privacy distance | anonymous recipients consuming on command |
| `Z6 — return and washing` | Used implements leave the clean route | separate return point, water, scrub/rinse/dry path, worker safety | invisible off-screen reset |
| `Z7 — records and translation` | Needs, shortages and errors are understandable and challengeable | interpreters, public quantities, correction route | modern bureaucracy copied without evidence |
| `Z8 — storage and replenishment` | Ordinary supplies remain visible around the inexhaustible food source | water, fuel, covers, spare tools, mats, medicine, ordinary ingredients | implication that the sacred vessel supplies every material need |
| `Z9 — animal care edge` | Nonhuman feeding is safe and separate | species-appropriate distance, water, keeper, waste path | animals mixed into human food or crowd danger |

Every public-feeding location must show at least `Z1`–`Z6`. Kanchi requires all
nine. A household threshold may compress zones but cannot collapse clean and
used paths into one action.

## Aadhirai household plate

### Required visible chain

1. stored food belongs to a functioning household, not a magically prepared set;
2. at least one preparation or transfer implement carries signs of ordinary use;
3. Aadhirai controls the threshold and opens her stores voluntarily;
4. the food enters Amudhasurabhi through two people's coordinated handling;
5. ghee or sheen is restrained and linked to the approved Venpa perspective,
   not advertised as a proven universal ancient practice;
6. the first public portion leaves only after the household action is complete.

### Open terms and objects

- `சோறு`: preparation and grain state remain under `SR-002`/`SR-007` review;
- `கறி`: must not automatically become a modern curry recipe;
- `பசுநெய்`: quantity, appearance and historical-material claim remain open;
- storage, cooking and serving vessel forms remain comparative reconstructions;
- food styling must record edible substitutions and allergens separately.

## Public service flow

```text
ARRIVAL
  ├─ urgent water / medical need → seated support → service brought to person
  ├─ standing service possible → drinking water → serving → eating/rest
  └─ language or record barrier → interpreter / correction → chosen route

USED ITEMS
  → separate return
  → scrape or dispose by approved production method
  → wash
  → rinse
  → dry
  → clean storage

SHORTAGE OR ERROR
  → worker or recipient reports
  → public record corrected
  → route / portion / supply changes
  → affected person receives follow-up
```

Camera blocking must allow movement in both directions. A queue cannot trap a
person who needs to leave, sit, reach a child or obtain water first.

## Accessibility and recipient agency

- Provide standing, seated and brought-to-person service heights.
- Let recipients point, refuse, divide, save, exchange or request correction.
- Keep families and chosen companions visible unless separation is a plot fact.
- Use interpreters as active service workers, not festival decoration.
- Do not identify disability only through costume or exaggerated movement.
- Show at least one former recipient becoming a worker without implying that
  food must be repaid.
- Animal care remains present but physically separated from human preparation,
  washing and eating paths.

## Clean / used route prohibition

The following may never share one unexamined surface:

- prepared food and removed restraints;
- clean serving tools and returned recipient vessels;
- drinking-water storage and washing wastewater;
- food-safe inserts and continuity paint/chemical ageing materials;
- human food preparation and animal feeding vessels.

The Sequence 04 prison concept frame is compositionally useful but places
removed chains prominently near converted activity. Department development
must move those objects to a witnessed decommissioning zone away from food,
water and clean tools.

   understandable only after human giving and serving.
