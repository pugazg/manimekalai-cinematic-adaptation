# Season and Water Continuity Calendar 0.1

## Status and limits

This production plate implements `PD-011` without assigning exact Gregorian
months, modern climate normals or one proven chronology to the epic. It is an
`[INTERPRETATION]` built from the famine, geography, architecture and water
systems dossiers (`03F`, `03G`, `03H`) and their registered evidence and
decisions. `SR-006` and `SR-010` remain open.

The calendar controls visible continuity, not historical certainty. Rainfall,
river flow, stored water, drinking access and food access are separate fields.
A wet surface does not prove safe drinking water; a full market does not prove
that every household can obtain food.

## Water-state vocabulary

| Code | Production meaning | Visible indicators | Prohibited inference |
|---|---|---|---|
| `W0 — SACRED / LIMINAL` | Water is physically present but its narrative function is memory, passage or entrusted responsibility | Shallow sea, salt edge, pond surface, carried drinking water | Sacred water automatically solves thirst or sanitation |
| `W1 — WORKING ABUNDANCE` | Water appears plentiful and supports labour, but access and maintenance remain visible | River traffic, carried jars, washing edge, drainage, wells or storage | Every resident has equal clean-water access |
| `W2 — CROWD STRAIN` | Normal systems are pressured by festival, migration, service or custody | Queues, replenishment, muddy edges, guarded storage, additional carriers | Crowd colour and spectacle can hide infrastructure |
| `W3 — CONTROLLED ACCESS` | Water exists but authority, doors, distance or records determine who receives it | Guarded container, measured ration, locked threshold, witness chain | Scarcity is purely natural |
| `W4 — SYSTEMIC FAILURE` | Multiple failures interact across source, storage, work, transport, price and access | Empty or guarded jars, dry channels, interrupted workshops, long routes | One dry well or one absent rain explains the famine |
| `W5 — DISTRIBUTED RECOVERY` | Water and food move through shared, auditable routes rather than one person | Local custodians, reopened work, cleaning, drinking stations, route maps | One rainfall, ruler or miraculous object permanently ends crisis |

## Location continuity cards

### Puhar

- Water appears close because the city is river-sea connected, but potable
  access is never assumed.
- Harbour water, household storage, garden moisture, public serving water and
  custody rations require different prop and colour states.
- Festival density raises demand before it becomes a plot statement.
- Repeated locations must retain marks of labour: damp carrying paths, jar
  rings, cleaning zones, repaired shade and drainage edges.

### Manipallavam

- Sea and pond are not one undifferentiated magical surface.
- Salt exposure, shallow island geography, wind and carried water remain
  physical even when memory disrupts ordinary time.
- The pond's sacred role does not authorize a specific modern-island copy or
  make every surrounding plant historically fixed.

### Vanji

- Inland water, shade and trade corridors distinguish it from tidal Puhar.
- The philosophical spaces remain connected to streets, lodging, food and
  carried water; they are not abstract white rooms.
- Travel into and out of Vanji begins the cumulative deterioration that leads
  to Kanchi.

### Kanchi

- Later Pallava monumentality is prohibited as an automatic backdrop.
- Famine is shown through access, work, storage, routes and prices as well as
  visibly reduced water.
- Recovery is geographically uneven. Relief centres may function while outer
  roads, workshops or households remain fragile.
- The final state is `W5`, not restored `W1`: systems are being rebuilt and
  audited, not returned magically to an untouched past.

## Department continuity fields

Every relevant scene or plate should record:

| Department | Required field |
|---|---|
| Locations / art | source, storage, access controller, drainage or waste path |
| Props | vessel ID, material family, capacity class, clean/used state, carrier |
| Costume | dry/damp/salt/soil state, heat and travel response, laundering continuity |
| Hair and makeup | heat, thirst, sleep, confinement and recovery progression without spectacle |
| Camera | whether water is geography, labour, controlled access, failure or recovery |
| Lighting | reflected humidity, dust and practical fire without sacred-light proof |
| Sound | source flow, carrying, pouring, queue, cleaning, drainage, absence and guarded silence |
| VFX | coastline or crowd extension only after practical access relationships are established |
| Animals | shade, drinking access, route pressure and welfare separation |
| Script supervision | sequence, day progression, water code and bodily consequence |

## Colour and surface progression

The progression is relational rather than a fixed grading LUT:

1. Puhar begins with humid gold, indigo shade and working colour.
2. Crowd strain increases reflected heat, trampled moisture and service wear.
3. Manipallavam separates salt white, pearl grey and shallow green without
   becoming an ethereal void.
4. Public feeding uses earthen food and vessel colours; cleanliness is visible
   through workflow, not polished surfaces.
5. Palace control narrows practical lamplight and access, not skin tone.
6. Kanchi loses colour unevenly through dust, interrupted dye work, reduced
   food and guarded water.
7. Recovery returns colour first through work, washed vessels, reopened
   materials and people moving safely—not through a supernatural sky change.

## Continuity prohibitions

- No rain montage may independently end Kanchi's famine.
- No wet street may be added only for cinematic reflection without a source
  and consequence.
- No “ancient city” may reuse identical wells, jars, drains and vegetation.
- No sacred pond may double automatically as drinking, bathing and ritual
  water.
- No recipient may be shown as restored by food without water, rest and time.
- No palace ration container may change shape or capacity between order,
  transport, withholding and witness scenes.
- No relief station may omit waste, washing or accessibility merely because
  Amudhasurabhi supplies food.
