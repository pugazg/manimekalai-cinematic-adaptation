# Guard, Custody and Weapon Handling Plate 0.1

## Status and evidence boundary

This plate implements the first `PD-010` department sheet. It coordinates
props, costume, stunt, blocking, locations, script supervision and institutional
continuity. It is `[INTERPRETATION]`, not proof of one exact early Tamil sword,
guard uniform, key, restraint, office or custody procedure.

`VU-018` and `VU-019` remain open. `SR-003`, `SR-006`, `SR-008`, `SR-009`,
`SR-012` and `SR-013` remain required before design or action lock.

## Dramatic rules

1. Weapons are tools with consequences, not automatic signs of honour.
2. Kanchanan's possession, concealment, waiting and strike remain choices.
3. Udayakumaran's death is not an equal duel, self-defence or accident.
4. Guards enact power through routes, doors, records, food and silence as much
   as through visible arms.
5. Uniformity must not invent a later standing bureaucracy or military system.
6. Custody is legible through who can enter, leave, reach objects, carry orders
   and refuse commands.
7. No hero shot, flourish, slow-motion fetish or publicity image may turn the
   killing into romantic masculine spectacle.

## Authority-state vocabulary

| Code | State | Visual behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| `G0 — civic presence` | guards or officials manage a route without immediate detention | perimeter, witness and exit remain visible; arms secondary |
| `G1 — monitored access` | entry, water, market or debate access is controlled | threshold and decision-maker visible; refusal has a traceable source |
| `G2 — formal custody` | named person is detained before witnesses | reason, escort, destination and possessions recorded; no gratuitous force |
| `G3 — concealed custody` | public record and private physical reality diverge | screen, door, guard rotation and object reach expose the lie |
| `G4 — delegated coercion` | harm occurs through attendants, rations, rumours and routine obedience | order chain remains visible without one theatrical villain guard |
| `G5 — witnessed release` | access and bodily control actually change in public | door, guards, possessions and exit route all resolve; statement alone is insufficient |
| `G6 — accountable service` | guards protect access rather than restrict recipients | crowd safety, fair gates, recorded decisions and appeal/correction route |

## Guard costume and equipment families

Do not build one “ancient Tamil guard uniform.” Distinguish function through
material wear, location, employer and task:

- palace threshold and witness guard;
- escort or detention guard;
- storage/market access guard;
- hired caravan guard;
- relief-route and crowd-safety guard.

Shared identity may use one restrained, evidence-neutral element after
`SR-006` review, but rank must not be communicated through fantasy armour.
Equipment should remain functional, repairable and compatible with heat,
walking, water carrying, door work and crowd movement.

## Weapons and stunt safety

- A qualified weapons and stunt lead controls every prop version and handoff.
- No sharpened or live-edge weapon is used in action or performer proximity.
- Hero, rehearsal, flexible-contact, retractable, drop and evidence versions
  receive distinct identifiers.
- Every draw, strike, fall and drop has an exclusion zone, cue and abort word.
- Wet/blood-effect surfaces are tested for grip and immediately controlled.
- Performers rehearse distance and eyelines before costumes, low light and
  fluid effects are introduced.
- The shortest safe action is preferred; emotional intensity never authorises
  uncontrolled speed or contact.
- Publicity photography may not pose Kanchanan with the fatal weapon as an
  aspirational hero image.

## Violence and trauma safeguards

- The camera does not occupy the blade's point of view.
- Manimekalai's disguise is not framed as causing the strike.
- Udayakumaran's prior pursuit and Kanchanan's grief remain causal context, not
  legal or moral excuses.
- Reaction and aftermath preserve Manimekalai's refusal and grief without
  retrospective consent.
- Rehearsal does not require performers to disclose personal experiences of
  violence, coercion or bereavement.
- Intensity may be built through breath, thresholds, incomplete information and
  consequence rather than repeated impact.
