# Production Design Bible 0.1

## Status

This is a production-facing synthesis of the research archive. It is not a claim of exact reconstruction. Every department must retain the distinction between literary evidence, comparative history, production interpretation and unresolved questions.

## Department-wide rules

1. Build systems, not isolated monuments.
2. Show labour, repair, water, storage, access and waste.
3. Do not use later monumental architecture as shorthand for antiquity.
4. Do not use modern Bharatanatyam costume as shorthand for a performing woman.
5. Do not let imported luxury erase local labour.
6. Do not turn a comparative archaeological find into a Puhar-specific fact.
7. Do not encode virtue through colourlessness or ornament through moral failure.
8. Do not use sacred light as proof.
9. Keep every supernatural object physically handled and materially consequential.
10. Record every unresolved visual decision before concept approval.

---

## PD-001 — Puhar urban system

### Design thesis

Puhar is a distributed river-sea city whose districts depend on one another. It should feel large because work, water, cargo, people and information move across it—not because every surface is monumental stone.

### Required zones

- Kaveri mouth and tidal working edge;
- river landing and timber transfer structures;
- small-craft channels and offshore-transfer sightlines;
- cargo sorting, grain handling and storage;
- occupational streets and repair yards;
- market interfaces;
- performance households and training spaces;
- civic routes used by the festival proclamation;
- royal and administrative access;
- public wells, water storage and drainage;
- gardens and quieter edges;
- Ulaga Aravi and care institutions;
- custody and prison spaces;
- Chakravala / cremation-ground transition landscape.

### Construction palette

- timber posts, beams, platforms and repair scaffolds;
- brick where evidence and function support it;
- compacted earth and mixed working surfaces;
- tiled, thatched and woven coverings;
- rope, mats, screens and awnings;
- clay and low-sheen metal vessels;
- drainage edges, wells and storage jars.

### Prohibited default

No continuous marble quay, axial fantasy capital, uniform paved street grid, medieval gopuram skyline or single “Roman quarter.”

### Continuity movement

At the beginning, Puhar appears connected but unequal. During public feeding, the same routes must reveal access constraints. After the prince’s death, doors and institutions turn those routes into custody. The city’s design therefore participates in the moral argument.

## PD-002 — Indra festival visual system

### Design thesis

The festival is first a civic-sacral institution of early Tamil Puhar: proclamation, preparation, rain/prosperity expectation, royal responsibility, multilingual gathering and debate. It is not automatically the same as later devotional iconography.

### Visible preparation

- workers clean routes and manage refuse;
- flower workers and textile workers prepare materials;
- animal keepers manage procession animals;
- musicians prepare rather than appear fully staged;
- merchants and visitors change street density;
- public speakers and schools occupy distinct listening spaces;
- water and food supply visibly strain under crowd scale.

### Procession rule

The procession must reveal who works, who watches, who is watched and who can move freely. The camera does not validate royal order merely by showing scale.

### Sacred rule

Do not invent a later iconic statue, vehicle or temple sequence as the festival’s definitive centre. Any ritual object remains `[INTERPRETATION]` until textual and historical review.

## PD-003 — Maritime vessel families

### Functional families

1. river and estuary work craft;
2. passenger and cargo transfer boats;
3. coastal craft;
4. larger ocean-going merchant silhouettes;
5. wreck fragments and survivor debris.

### Screen rule

Close views belong to functions we can defend: loading, balance, rope work, transfer, provisioning, repair, bailing and weather response. Larger ocean-going hulls remain visually cautious until `SR-010`.

### Prohibited default

No European square rig, classical Mediterranean galley, fantasy galleon or universal stitched-boat claim. A sewn-plank comparison cannot become an exact Puhar reconstruction.

## PD-004 — Architecture and water

### Water is architecture

Every major environment must answer:

- where drinking water comes from;
- how it is carried and stored;
- how cooking and cleaning obtain water;
- how rain and waste leave;
- who controls access;
- what fails during famine or confinement.

### Location contrast

| Location | Spatial quality | Water condition |
|---|---|---|
| Puhar | tidal, distributed, crowded, occupational | abundant-looking but managed and unequal |
| Manipallavam | sparse, shallow, exposed | sacred pond and surrounding sea are distinct |
| Vanji | inland corridor, administrative and intellectual | river-linked without copying Puhar |
| Kanchi | dense relief geography under famine pressure | scarcity, failed access and restored systems |

### Prohibited default

No unverified aqueduct, modern sewer logic, decorative “ancient” well without function or later Pallava monument as automatic Kanchi.

## PD-005 — Costume and textiles

### Costume families

- harbour and cargo labour;
- grain, flower, craft and market work;
- elite and royal households;
- trained performance household;
- travellers and merchants;
- teachers and philosophical communities;
- Buddhist renunciants;
- guards and animal keepers;
- famine-affected households;
- relief workers and recipients.

### Character arcs

**Manimekalai:** artistic-household memory remains in posture and movement. Her clothing becomes more practical through service and travel, but the arc is not “colourful woman becomes colourless saint.”

**Madhavi:** renunciation changes function and social relation without erasing trained bodily intelligence.

**Sudhamathi:** route-aware, practical clothing must support movement and vigilance.

**Udayakumaran:** access and privilege appear through material quality and attendants, not fantasy armour.

**Rajamadevi:** political authority is visible without making grief a costume spectacle.

### Prohibited default

No modern fan costume, one temple-jewellery package, pan-Indian fantasy drape or fixed robe colour presented as exact.

## PD-006 — Jewellery and personal ornament

### Material possibilities

Use shell, glass, terracotta, gold wire, beads and semi-precious stone only within the limits established by the archaeological dossiers.

### Movement test

Every ornament must be tested for:

- work;
- dance training;
- walking and travel;
- custody and sleeping;
- food service;
- sound;
- continuity and removal.

### Ethical rule

Adornment never signals consent, available sexuality or inferior morality.

## PD-007 — Food, vessels and service

### Aadhirai sequence

The production order is:

1. stored household ingredients and preparation;
2. Aadhirai’s voluntary decision;
3. prepared food enters Amudhasurabhi;
4. Manimekalai serves first portions;
5. the food level does not fall;
6. public feeding creates water, queue, access, cleaning and translation work.

### Public service stations

- arrival and triage without humiliation;
- drinking water;
- serving;
- shaded eating/rest;
- vessel return and washing;
- accessible route;
- children and separated families;
- animal feeding at a safe edge;
- storage and replenishment;
- visible correction of records or quantities.

### Prohibited default

No definitive menu, modern buffet, bottomless glowing food, jewel cauldron or crowd organised by magic.

---

## PD-008 — Amudhasurabhi

### Object thesis

Amudhasurabhi is a portable entrusted service vessel. It is important because food continues through giving, not because it dominates the frame.

### Continuity fields

- custodian;
- carrier;
- vessel orientation and hand position;
- clean / soiled state;
- food state;
- first visible fullness;
- first visible inexhaustibility;
- public custody;
- concealment or disguise context;
- storage and transfer;
- final shared-system relationship.

### VFX rule

The food level remains after portions are served. Avoid internal light, levitation, expanding geometry, magical steam or automatic self-serving.

## PD-009 — Music and performance

### Acoustic worlds

- harbour work rhythms;
- festival preparation and procession;
- Madhavi’s silent or interrupted performance household;
- philosophical speech and listening;
- palace grief and private orders;
- public feeding and vessel sounds;
- island wind and sparse water;
- famine workshops falling silent;
- relief work regaining layered sound.

### Prohibited default

No generic “ancient devotional” score over every sacred scene. Instruments, rhythm, voice and performance context remain research-open.

---

## PD-010 — Weapons and guards

### Design thesis

Weapons are tools whose presence changes access and consequence. They are not character-development jewellery.

### Kanchanan

His weapon must:

- require intentional handling;
- be visible before the strike without fetishistic coverage;
- preserve alternatives he could have chosen;
- remain physically consequential afterward.

### Custody

Guards, keys, doors, ration control, records and delegated orders matter more than a fantasy arsenal.

---

## PD-011 — Ecology and seasonal continuity

### Continuity bands

1. humid delta festival;
2. coastal travel and island exposure;
3. inland movement;
4. abundance in Savaka;
5. roads transmitting famine news;
6. Kanchi scarcity;
7. water and livelihood recovery.

### Rule

Recovery is not represented by one miraculous rain cut. Water, work, storage, price, transport, health and access change at different speeds.

---

## PD-012 — Animal work and care

### Animal roles

- Aputhiran’s protecting cow;
- animals threatened by ritual violence;
- elephants and horses in civic and elite systems;
- draft and transport animals;
- weakened animals on famine roads;
- animals receiving food at relief centres.

### Welfare rules

- no real fear, striking, dangerous restraint or forced collapse;
- use trained behaviour, separated plates, props and compositing;
- animal point-of-view is consequence, not comic revenge;
- carers and keepers remain visible;
- crowd and sound exposure require welfare planning.

## Approval sequence

1. department concept;
2. evidence and uncertainty check;
3. linked specialist review;
4. rights and reference-image review;
5. movement / handling test;
6. continuity sign-off;
7. storyboard integration;
8. production lock.

No plate moves directly from generated concept to production lock.
