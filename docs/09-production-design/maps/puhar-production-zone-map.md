# Puhar Production Zone Map

## Status

The companion SVG is a **production relationship diagram**, not a geographic reconstruction, archaeological site plan or claim about exact orientation, scale or street layout.

## Purpose

The map helps departments track:

- labour and cargo movement;
- water dependencies;
- public and restricted access;
- character routes;
- festival pressure;
- feeding and custody transformations;
- visual transitions between the river-sea edge and quieter margins.

## Evidence basis

- `03A_Puhar_City_Dossier`
- `03C_maritime_trade_and_ships`
- `03H_architecture_water_systems_and_urban_infrastructure`
- `03I_economy_labour_trade_guilds_and_material_culture`
- linked `EV-*`, `AD-*` and `SC-*` records

## Reading the diagram

- solid arrows: frequent material or labour relationship;
- blue arrows: water dependency;
- dashed arrows: story route or restricted transition;
- red boundary: custody or controlled-access function;
- grey outer band: spatial relationship unresolved.

No distance may be measured from the SVG.
