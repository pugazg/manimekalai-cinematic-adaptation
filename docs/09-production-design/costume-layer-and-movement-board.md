# Costume Layer and Movement Board 0.1

**Control family:** `PD-005`

**Specialist gate:** `SR-011` — early historic textiles, ornament and dress

**Status:** `[INTERPRETATION]` — **NOT EXACT SANGAM DRESS**

## Purpose

This board defines costume by occupation, movement, access and character state. It does not reconstruct one universal ancient Tamil outfit.

## Layer codes

| Code | Layer | Control question |
|---|---|---|
| `L0` | body preparation | oil, grooming, kohl and weathering: textual, practical or invented? |
| `L1` | primary drape | can the wearer work, walk, sit and respond to wind safely? |
| `L2` | secondary wrap | warmth, modesty, status, labour or visual invention? |
| `L3` | securing system | tie, fold, cord, belt or pin; no hidden modern fastening by default |
| `L4` | work/protection layer | carrying, dust, water, heat and tool interaction |
| `L5` | public/status layer | quality and maintenance rather than automatic volume or gold |
| `L6` | flowers/temporary dress | time, fragrance, decay, labour and replacement visible |
| `L7` | role/renunciation change | subtraction and reuse without inventing a standard uniform |

## Costume families

| Family | Required movement test | Prohibited shortcut |
|---|---|---|
| `C1 — Harbour labour` | lift, squat, carry, wet edge, secure loose cloth | decorative “peasant” distress |
| `C2 — Merchant household` | sit, account, receive goods, move through stores | generic luxury sari or all-gold styling |
| `C3 — Performance household` | turn, floor work, breath, rehearsal, ornament sound | modern Bharatanatyam fan costume |
| `C4 — Royal court` | controlled pace, seated procedure, guarded thresholds | fantasy crown or medieval imperial default |
| `C5 — Teacher / ascetic` | walking, sitting, alms, weather, repeated wear | standardized later robe without terminology review |
| `C6 — Public service` | carry vessel, serve, wash, bend, crowd protection | spotless symbolic costume disconnected from labour |
| `C7 — Travel / island` | salt, wind, sleep, fatigue and re-draping | pristine continuity after exposure |
| `C8 — Famine / repair` | altered adult garments, patch, sale and reuse | anonymous rags applied equally to everyone |

## Character continuity

### Manimekalai

Her path is `C3 → C7 → C5/C6`, with traces of trained movement retained. Reduced ornament does not erase body memory, beauty, fear or agency. No shaved head, fixed robe colour or exact garment construction is approved without source and institutional review.

### Madhavi

Her performance knowledge remains in posture, storage and cloth handling after public withdrawal. Renunciation is not modern widow styling and cannot be reduced to removing jewellery.

### Sudhamathi

Her clothing supports practical route reading and quick movement without turning her into a visually “plain” servant beside Manimekalai.

### Rajamadevi and Chitrapathi

Authority appears through material quality, maintenance, controlled repetition and access. Their power is not measured by costume mass.

## Required tests

Each approved look receives:

1. `M1` — standing and walking silhouette;
2. `M2` — floor sitting and rising;
3. `M3` — work/tool interaction;
4. `M4` — wind, water and heat response;
5. `M5` — crowd and safety clearance;
6. `M6` — sound with ornament removed and added;
7. `M7` — continuity after travel, grief, confinement or service;
8. `M8` — camera test proving it does not read as a later familiar costume package.

## Binding prohibitions

- no modern Bharatanatyam fan costume;
- no modern bridal package for Manimekalai;
- no generic saffron/colourless nun shortcut;
- no medieval or imperial Chola default;
- no highly tailored modern garment logic without disclosure;
- no exact dye, fibre, weave or garment-name claim without evidence;
- no publicity description of any design as “exact Sangam dress.”

## Approval path

Build low-cost drape tests for `C1`–`C8`, run `M1`–`M8`, then submit textile hypotheses, fastenings, movement footage and terminology to `SR-011`.
