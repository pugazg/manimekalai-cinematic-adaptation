# Amudhasurabhi Prop and Continuity Plate 0.1

## Status and evidence boundary

This plate implements the next `PD-008` deliverable. It is a production
engineering and continuity system, not an assertion that the epic fixes one
exact material, diameter, profile or carrying method. The working object
remains `[INTERPRETATION]`; `VU-015` and `VU-016` remain open under `SR-003`,
`SR-004` and `SR-007`, with costume/movement review under `SR-011` and rights
review under `SR-013`.

The prop must communicate four truths:

1. it is portable and physically handled;
2. its contents continue, but the object does not perform service by itself;
3. custody carries responsibility rather than ownership;
4. food continuity never removes water, cleaning, access or labour.

## Prototype envelope

The art and props departments should build two neutral rehearsal shells before
fixing a hero profile. These dimensions are ergonomic tests, not historical
claims.

| Prototype | Test envelope | Intended question | Reject if |
|---|---|---|---|
| `P-A — close carry` | opening approximately 28–30 cm; depth 13–15 cm; empty rehearsal mass 1.2–1.8 kg | Can Manimekalai hold it close, travel, turn and serve repeatedly without making it ornamental? | It reads as a small domestic cup, permits one-handed superhero handling or hides food-level continuity |
| `P-B — public service` | opening approximately 33–36 cm; depth 15–18 cm; empty rehearsal mass 1.8–2.6 kg | Can two-handed serving, household filling and shared custody remain legible in medium and wide frames? | It reads as an immovable cauldron, requires implausible strength or blocks the actor's face and relationships |

The final hero, stunt, lightweight, wet-work and food-safe inserts must share
one visible silhouette. Mass may vary for safety, but performance must preserve
credible weight and inertia.

## Material families to test

| Family | Screen value | Risk and unresolved question |
|---|---|---|
| low-sheen metal shell | durable hero surface; catches a narrow line of food or fire light | alloy, manufacture and exact period association remain open; avoid polished treasure coding |
| fired-clay or clay-finish shell | connects the object to ordinary food vessels and labour | breakage, wet work and making the sacred vessel indistinguishable from every container |
| composite production shell with evidence-neutral finish | permits safe repeats, travel, water and actor handling | must not be described publicly as the ancient material |

No jewels, internal light source, autonomous lid movement, levitation, expanding
geometry or permanently clean sacred surface.

## Handling vocabulary

| Code | Action | Performance meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `H0 — discovered` | hands test rim, base and balance before lifting | obligation is unfamiliar; revelation does not create mastery |
| `H1 — close carry` | base supported near the torso with the second hand stabilising rim or side | protection and physical responsibility, not possession as treasure |
| `H2 — receiving` | vessel supported while another person adds prepared food | giver and custodian share the action; Aadhirai retains household agency |
| `H3 — serving` | base supported; measured serving performed without tilting into magical self-service | repetitive labour and recipient relation remain visible |
| `H4 — transfer` | both custodians visibly share weight before one releases | custody change is deliberate and auditable |
| `H5 — withheld / unreachable` | object remains visible but outside the custodian's physical reach | institutional power controls access without changing moral responsibility |
| `H6 — shared station` | vessel rests at working height and may travel to someone unable to reach the queue | the system, not a throne or saint, governs access |

The actor should never mime weightlessness. Fatigue may alter grip, elbow
height, stance and frequency of rest without implying the food itself becomes
finite.

## Food-level states

| Code | Visible state | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `F0 — empty / unknown` | interior readable but without treasure or glow | discovery, teaching and pre-offering uncertainty |
| `F1 — human first fullness` | Aadhirai's prepared rice and accompaniment fill the visible working level | establishes that ordinary generosity precedes visible inexhaustibility |
| `F2 — first portions removed` | portions leave through ordinary serving action | camera remains with hands and recipients, not the interior |
| `F3 — unchanged return` | on looking back, the level matches `F1` without refill, light or morphing | first visual disclosure of continuing abundance |
| `F4 — sustained service` | continuity inserts repeat a defendable level across servings | large-scale feeding; avoid bottomless-vortex imagery |
| `F5 — covered transit` | food-safe cover or cloth is practical, non-magical and continuity-tracked | roads, crowd movement and contamination control |

The first miracle beat is `F2 → F3`, not the vessel's appearance. Every later
scene inherits that grammar.

## Cleanliness and working-state codes

| Code | State | Required action |
|---|---|---|
| `C0 — dry / newly received` | pond or discovery continuity resolved; no decorative spotless shine | inspect before use |
| `C1 — prepared for food` | clean insert and serving tools established | hands and work surface checked |
| `C2 — active service` | restrained food marks at rim or implement contact | rotate tools, wipe safely, protect recipients |
| `C3 — post-service` | used state visible; vessel requires cleaning even if contents are sacred | washing labour and water source shown or accounted for |
| `C4 — custody / storage` | dry, covered and logged without becoming a shrine display | named or rotating custodian; accessible emergency path |

No reset to immaculate `C0` between shots unless cleaning or elapsed time is
established.

The orientation mark must be invisible to camera or evidence-neutral. Do not
invent a sacred inscription merely to solve prop continuity.

## Camera and VFX tests

1. Shoot `F1`, three ordinary servings and `F3` with a locked interior angle.
2. Test whether a practical replaceable food insert can preserve grain and
   accompaniment placement without digital boiling, glow or morph.
3. Test `H2` and `H4` with performers of different heights so shared weight is
   legible and safe.
4. Test walking, sitting, threshold crossing and crowd turns with `P-A` and
   `P-B` before selecting size.
5. Test a lightweight stunt shell while preserving the hero shell's inertia.
6. Test cleaning, food-safety and replacement time under public-serving
   continuity.
7. Use VFX only for invisible continuity support or portion reset; the audience
   should discover inexhaustibility by comparison, not witness an effect show.

## Safety and food handling

- Use food-safe removable inserts for edible material.
- Separate hero finish, rehearsal shell, stunt shell and wet-work shell.
- Set maximum repeated-carry and serving durations with the performer and
  movement team.
- Provide rest positions that can be incorporated into blocking.
- Never require an actor to lift a heavy filled hero shell repeatedly for the
  sake of “authentic weight.”
- Track allergens, temperature, spoilage and reset food independently of the
  fictional inexhaustibility.
