# Remaining Specialist Candidate Slate

_Public-profile verification date: 2026-08-03_

## Purpose and limits

This slate applies the non-assignment and privacy rules in
`critical-review-candidate-slate.md` to the nine remaining specialist tracks,
`SR-004` through `SR-012`. It records public professional evidence from official
institutional pages and relevant scholarship already registered in the
repository. It does not claim contact, willingness, conflict clearance,
appointment or approval. Every authoritative assignment remains `UNASSIGNED`.

The labels `STRONG_PUBLIC_PROFILE_MATCH`, `PARTIAL_MATCH_REFERRAL_ONLY`,
`WORK_BASED_LEAD_STATUS_UNVERIFIED` and `INSTITUTIONAL_REFERRAL_REQUIRED` have
the meanings defined in the critical slate. This document adds
`JOINT_REVIEW_REQUIRED` where no responsible single-person review can cover the
gate's deliberately combined disciplines.
