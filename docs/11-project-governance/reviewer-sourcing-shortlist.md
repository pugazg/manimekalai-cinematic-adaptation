# Reviewer Sourcing Shortlist

_Prepared: 2026-08-03_

## Status and limits

This document supplies public institutional referral routes for the thirteen
external-review tracks. It does **not** name, appoint, endorse or imply the
willingness of any reviewer. Every row in
`external-review-assignment-register.csv` remains `UNASSIGNED` until a person:

1. is checked against `reviewer-selection-rubric.csv`;
2. receives the applicable review packet;
3. declares relevant conflicts;
4. accepts the bounded scope and evidence conditions; and
5. gives permission for the public name or affiliation recorded in the
   assignment register.

Institutional offices may decline, may refer the project elsewhere, and may
not provide private recommendations. Public contact details must not be copied
into the assignment register unless they belong to the eventual reviewer and
the reviewer has consented to their storage. This shortlist should be
re-verified immediately before outreach because staff and contact routes can
change.

## Actor-table-read sourcing

The Tamil table read is not satisfied by appointing `SR-001` alone. The project
needs adult Tamil-speaking performers who can read the roles without being
presented as historical or doctrinal authorities. The National School of Drama
Bengaluru Centre is a reasonable public referral route because it serves theatre
aspirants across the southern states, including Tamil Nadu and Puducherry.
Outreach must use `tamil-table-read-protocol.md`, explain recording and privacy
choices before the session and obtain participant consent before publishing any
name, audio, timing note or quotation.

## Outreach order

1. Contact `SR-001`, `SR-002` and `SR-003` referral routes first. These reviews
   affect dialogue, source fidelity and the implemented Venpa material.
2. In parallel, seek `SR-004`–`SR-007` referrals for doctrine, philosophy,
   history and food practice.
3. Pair `SR-008` and `SR-009` with the actor-table-read planning so ethical
   performance notes are available before the read.
4. Seek `SR-010`–`SR-012` before expanding storyboard anchors or department
   plates in their affected domains.
5. Seek `SR-013` before any change to Option B, any licence badge, or any claim
   that public visibility grants reuse rights.

## Assignment transition

After a person responds, do not overwrite `UNASSIGNED` immediately. First make
a private intake record, verify the rubric, receive the conflict declaration and
scope acceptance, and confirm what identity information may be public. Only then
record `INVITED`, `ACCEPTED` or another state defined by
`external-review-execution-protocol.md`. A referral email, institutional reply or
informal conversation is not an approval result.
