# Critical Review Candidate Slate

_Public-profile verification date: 2026-08-03_

## Purpose and non-assignment rule

This slate narrows the four `CRITICAL` specialist gates (`SR-001`, `SR-002`,
`SR-003` and `SR-013`) from institutional routes to public-profile leads. It is
an outreach-preparation record, not an appointment record. No person listed
here has been contacted by this project, accepted work, declared conflicts,
agreed to publication of their identity or reviewed any repository material.
The authoritative assignment register therefore remains `UNASSIGNED`.

The slate records only professional information displayed on official
institutional pages. It intentionally omits personal telephone numbers, direct
email addresses and inferred private details. A profile match authorizes an
invitation, not a claim of endorsement, availability or approval.

## Readiness labels

- `STRONG_PUBLIC_PROFILE_MATCH`: the official profile demonstrates the core
  subject competence, but conflict, scope, evidence access and willingness are
  still unverified.
- `PARTIAL_MATCH_REFERRAL_ONLY`: the official profile supports one part of the
  gate; the person must not be assigned as the sole reviewer on that evidence.
- `WORK_BASED_LEAD_STATUS_UNVERIFIED`: an official institutional record ties
  the person to directly relevant work, but present affiliation, contact route
  or full rubric fit is not established.
- `INSTITUTIONAL_REFERRAL_REQUIRED`: no named person is sufficiently supported
  by current official evidence; ask the institution to nominate a qualified
  reviewer.
