# External Review Outreach Batch

_Prepared: 2026-08-03. No invitation recorded as sent._

## Purpose and authority limit

This is the execution bridge between the public candidate slates and the
authoritative assignment register. It routes all thirteen specialist gates to
bounded packets without treating research, invitation or silence as review.
It does not authorize an external message, promise compensation, publish
private contact information or change any `UNASSIGNED` row.

Before sending any item, the owner or coordinator must:

1. reverify the official affiliation and contact route on the day of contact;
2. choose a real person or ask the named institution for a qualified referral;
3. use the general invitation in
   [`specialist-review-packets/reviewer-invitation-and-intake.md`](specialist-review-packets/reviewer-invitation-and-intake.md);
4. attach only the packet and lawful sources listed below;
5. record private contact and compensation information outside Git;
6. update the assignment register only after written acceptance, conflict
   disclosure, accepted scope and public-credit preference are known.

## Send-time scope clauses

Insert the relevant clause after the general invitation's opening paragraphs.
These supplement rather than replace the packet-specific paragraphs.

### `SR-001`

Please review the complete Tamil screenplay as spoken performance language,
not only as written literary Tamil. Distinguish semantic correction,
actor-facing improvement and personal stylistic preference.

### `SR-002`

Please verify only the identified Bharathidasan-derived uses. Each finding must
name the edition and page image consulted; OCR alone is not an acceptable basis
for dialogue lock.

### `SR-003`

Please identify whether each flagged movement is directly supported by the
primary epic, supported in function but cinematically rephrased, or a later
interpretation. Record the Tamil edition and stable locator for every blocking
or major finding.

### `SR-004` and `SR-005`

Please treat Buddhist doctrine and rival philosophical schools as distinct
scopes. Competing claims should be intelligible without dramatic victory or
private revelation substituting for argument.

### `SR-006` and `SR-010`–`SR-012`

Please classify each claim as direct evidence, responsible comparison,
plausible reconstruction or prohibited certainty. Evidence from one site,
period or craft must not be transferred silently to all early Tamilakam.

### `SR-007`

Please separate cultivated ingredients, prepared food, vessel or serving
practice and cinematic interpretation. The Aadhirai offering must not be
marketed as one archaeologically proven ancient recipe.

### `SR-008` and `SR-009`

Please review consent, survivor agency and coercive harm without diagnosing
characters or performers. Do not request personal trauma disclosure; preserve
each woman's narrative agency.

### `SR-013`

Option B—the fully reserved interim state—is already implemented. Please state
the jurisdiction and advisory capacity of the response, identify required
corrections, and distinguish present advice from conditions for a future
licensing change.

## Batch acceptance rule

The batch is ready to send when all thirteen rows have a reverified route and
the owner has approved external contact. It is complete only when every gate
has a documented outcome under the execution protocol. The present state is
**prepared, not sent, unassigned and not specialist approved**.
