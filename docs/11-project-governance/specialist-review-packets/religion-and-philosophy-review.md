# Religion and Philosophy Review

## Scope

This packet covers `SR-004` and `SR-005`. It requires reviewers with competence in Tamil Buddhist literature, Buddhist doctrine and the history of Indian philosophical argument.

## Controlled materials

- `docs/05-philosophy/`
- Aravana Adigal and Manimekalai character bibles
- Sequences `03`, `04`, `08`, `09` and `10` in both languages
- `tamil-terminology-register.csv`, especially `TERM-TA-006`, `018`–`023`
- evidence and adaptation decisions touching karma, rebirth, causation, inference, Peedihai, renunciation and final practice

## Buddhist-studies questions (`SR-004`)

1. Does previous-birth memory remain sacred narrative experience without being presented as universally sufficient public proof?
2. Does the screenplay distinguish karmic explanation from legal blame?
3. Are causation and dependent-origination ideas intelligible without becoming a modern systems-theory substitution?
4. Is renunciation shown as disciplined practice rather than emotional extinction or self-erasure?
5. Is Aputhiran’s final fast treated with textual seriousness without being promoted as a replicable ideal?
6. Is Aravana Adigal explicitly Buddhist while remaining a teacher whose claims may be questioned?
7. Does compassion remain bodily, institutional and intellectual rather than merely serene?
8. Does the final practice avoid claiming that one miracle or individual permanently solves suffering?

For every disputed term, provide the Tamil form, transliteration where useful, literal range, performance-ready option and subtitle-ready option.

## Indian-philosophy questions (`SR-005`)

1. Are the rival positions presented in their strongest intelligible form?
2. Are `ஆசீவக`, `நிகண்டவாத`, `வைசேடிக` and `பூதவாதி` responsible labels for the intended screenplay functions?
3. Should `பொருள்முதல்வாதி` appear as explanation, subtitle gloss or not at all?
4. Does the materialist challenge distinguish private experience from public evidence?
5. Are perception, inference, example, fallacy and correction used consistently?
6. Does the screenplay avoid importing later systematised school boundaries as unquestioned historical fact?
7. Is Chapter 29’s reasoning material connected responsibly to, but not falsely identified with, later logic traditions?
8. Does dramatic compression preserve genuine disagreement rather than producing a debate Manimekalai simply “wins”?

## Acceptance gate

Approval requires:

- no unresolved doctrinal error;
- all contested terminology recorded;
- uncertainty retained where scholarship is unsettled;
- matched English/Tamil corrections;
- a final check that philosophical revisions still function dramatically;
- passing repository validation.
