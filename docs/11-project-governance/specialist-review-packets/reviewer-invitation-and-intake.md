# Reviewer Invitation and Intake Kit

## Purpose

This document turns a selected candidate into a bounded review assignment.
It is a reusable invitation, not evidence that anyone has been contacted or
has accepted.

Before sending, compare the candidate with
[`reviewer-selection-rubric.csv`](../reviewer-selection-rubric.csv). Keep
private email addresses outside the public repository.

## General invitation

**Subject:** Specialist review request — *Manimekalai* cinematic adaptation

Dear [Reviewer name],

I am developing a source-traceable English–Tamil cinematic adaptation of the
Tamil epic *Manimekalai*. The project separates primary text, historical
evidence, later literary perspectives and cinematic interpretation, and it
does not treat internal review or automated validation as specialist
approval.

I would like to invite you to review the bounded scope identified as
`[SR-###] — [discipline]`. The attached or linked packet identifies the
specific documents, scenes, terminology, evidence and questions. We are not
asking you to approve the complete film or areas outside your expertise.

Please confirm:

1. whether the scope fits your qualifications;
2. any part you exclude;
3. any conflict of interest;
4. the editions or evidence you can lawfully consult;
5. whether you can provide a dated written response using the supplied
   template;
6. your expected schedule and whether acknowledgement or compensation is
   required.

The repository currently uses an Option B fully reserved interim rights
state. Source scans and protected editions will not be published or shared
without lawful access. Your review will be credited publicly only with your
permission.

Thank you,

[Owner or coordinator]

## Packet-specific scope paragraph

Choose the matching paragraph and insert it after the invitation's second
paragraph.

### Tamil, textual and performance — `SR-001`–`SR-003`, `SR-007`–`SR-009`

The review covers spoken Tamil, primary and Bharathidasan textual
compatibility, food terminology, consent, survivor agency and trauma-aware
performance. Please separate textual correction, performance preference and
ethical concern.

### Religion and philosophy — `SR-004`–`SR-005`

The review covers Buddhist causation, rebirth, renunciation and liberation,
as well as the strongest intelligible presentation of rival schools,
perception and inference. Please identify what is doctrine, historical
reconstruction or dramatic simplification.

### History and material culture — `SR-006`, `SR-010`–`SR-012`

The review covers institutions, maritime staging, costume and textiles,
ritual context and animal welfare. Please distinguish supported form,
responsible comparison, plausible interpretation and prohibited certainty.

### Rights and licensing — `SR-013`

The owner selected Option B, a fully reserved interim state. Please review
the root and per-directory notices, contribution terms, third-party
exclusions and visual-asset states, and identify required corrections before
any future split or open licence.

## Intake checklist

Do not change an assignment from `UNASSIGNED` until all applicable fields are
known:

- [ ] reviewer meets the selection rubric;
- [ ] public credit preference recorded privately;
- [ ] conflict declaration received;
- [ ] accepted and excluded scope recorded;
- [ ] source access is lawful;
- [ ] compensation or acknowledgement terms agreed;
- [ ] target date accepted;
- [ ] packet and response template supplied;
- [ ] private contact details kept outside Git;
- [ ] assignment register updated to `ASSIGNED` or `IN_REVIEW` only after acceptance.

## Returned-response intake

1. Preserve the response unchanged under a dated stable path.
2. Confirm that evidence locators and consulted editions are present.
3. Assign stable finding IDs.
4. Separate blocking, major, minor and advisory findings.
5. Record uncertainty and excluded scope.
6. Update English and Tamil together when meaning changes.
7. Return revised material to the same reviewer when approval conditions
   require it.
8. Run all validators, deterministic builds and the exhaustive audit.
9. Record the final repository disposition; do not silently delete rejected
   or superseded findings.

## Non-response rule

No response, an informal compliment, a social-media message or a verbal
conversation without a preserved scope and finding record does not close a
specialist gate.
