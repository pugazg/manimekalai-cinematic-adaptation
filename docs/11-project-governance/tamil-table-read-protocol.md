# Tamil Table-Read and Spoken-Performance Protocol

## Purpose

Internal language review has been completed for all ten Tamil sequences.
This protocol closes the separate actor-led gate without treating silent
reading, automated timing or structural parity as performance evidence.

## Required participants

- Tamil dialogue editor or screenwriter (`SR-001`);
- performers for Manimekalai, Madhavi, Sudhamathi, Udayakumaran, Aravana
  Adigal, Rajamadevi and the principal branch-story roles;
- session reader for action lines;
- continuity recorder;
- consent- and trauma-sensitive dramaturgy observer for the relevant scenes.

One performer may read multiple minor parts, but principal opposing voices
should not be collapsed when evaluating character distinction.

## Session plan

| Session | Sequences | Primary checks |
|---|---|---|
| `TR-01` | 01–02 | Puhar register, Madhavi–Manimekalai difference, Sudhamathi's practical voice, explicit refusal |
| `TR-02` | 03–04 | sacred language, Aputhiran branch clarity, Aadhirai food terms, public-service logistics |
| `TR-03` | 05–07 | pursuit versus consent, Kanchanan inference, grief, confinement, Rajamadevi accountability |
| `TR-04` | 08–10 | rebirth continuity, philosophical schools, inference vocabulary, Kanchi relief language |
| `TR-05` | complete feature | transitions, cumulative repetition, total spoken duration and emotional continuity |

## Recording form

For every scene record:

- sequence and scene number;
- participating roles;
- first-read duration;
- repeated-read duration if changed;
- line that felt translated rather than spoken;
- unclear referent or agency;
- tongue-twister or breath problem;
- terminology requiring explanation;
- character-voice collision;
- unintended comic, romantic, devotional or accusatory reading;
- proposed bilingual correction;
- reviewer disposition.

## Non-negotiable safeguards

1. Do not improve fluency by weakening Manimekalai's refusal.
2. Do not make previous-life memory sound like present consent.
3. Do not turn Kanchanan's inference failure into fate or absolution.
4. Do not turn Rajamadevi's recognition into automatic forgiveness.
5. Do not make Aputhiran's fast an instruction or triumphant ideal.
6. Do not reduce philosophical rivals to jokes or exposition props.
7. Do not remove food, water, access, sanitation or labour language merely
   to accelerate the relief sequences.
8. Do not alter only Tamil when the meaning change affects the English master.

## Timing rule

The 165-minute architecture is a target, not proof of spoken duration. Record
real read times, pauses, movement allowances and branch transitions. A timing
change becomes authoritative only after its scene-level effect is entered in
the architecture and both language editions remain aligned.

## Closure evidence

The actor-led gate closes only when:

- `TR-01`–`TR-05` are completed and dated;
- participant roles and review authority are recorded;
- all blocking language and performance findings are resolved;
- resulting English–Tamil changes pass the validators and release rebuild;
- `SR-001`, `SR-008` and `SR-009` dispositions are updated where applicable.
