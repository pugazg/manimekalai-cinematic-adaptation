## Generation brief

Show a former royal prison becoming a public care and feeding institution in
one relational frame: doors open, removed restraints collected visibly, water
storage established, food prepared, sleeping mats placed, records made and a
former prisoner receiving the first ladle as a participant. Manimekalai may
facilitate but must not dominate the composition as a solitary saviour.

Use a participant-height wide lens, late-afternoon warmth entering shadowed
spaces, low-sheen vessels, clay water storage and layered draped cloth. Avoid
torture spectacle, fantasy palace architecture, modern school staging and a
glowing Amudhasurabhi.

## Internal reading

The frame makes the reform legible as a spatial system:

`opened threshold → water → kitchen → lodging → record → dignified service`.

## Remaining uncertainty

The tiled roofs, masonry, heavy chain forms, cell doors, writing implements,
food vessels and garments are provisional. They are not evidence for exact
early Tamil penal architecture or administration. Historical, institutional,
