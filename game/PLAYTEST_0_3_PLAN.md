# G2 Prototype 0.3 — Playtest Plan

Prototype: G2 Prototype 0.3 — Meaningful Agency
Status: **Not yet playtested.** This is the plan, prepared before any owner/child play.

> This document does **not** record results. No answers are pre-filled, no participant
> names/ages/counts/quotes/ratings are invented, and nothing here claims validation.
> Results, if gathered, will be written up separately and honestly, the way the 0.2
> record was.

## What 0.3 is trying to learn

The central question:

> **Does the player feel they are making meaningful choices, or merely discovering the
> developer's intended solution?**

The secondary question:

> **After 20–25 minutes, does the player want to keep playing?**

Success is **not** "there are more branches." Success is: *did the player feel the
outcome reflected how they chose to help?*

## How to run it

1. Let the player play a full session (target 20–25 minutes) without coaching. If a
   child plays, let them explore; don't steer their order of actions.
2. Do not explain the "right" way — there isn't one. If they get stuck on *how to
   interact* (not *what to choose*), that is a clarity finding worth noting.
3. Afterwards, ask the questions below in a relaxed way. Record only what the player
   actually says.
4. The end screen shows a local "playtest summary" (first priority, who was spoken to,
   whether they served early, helpers reassigned, who continued the work). It is for
   your observation only — no score, no telemetry, no network. Use "Copy playtest
   summary" if you want to keep it with your notes.

## Planned owner/child debrief questions

1. Who did you choose to help first? Why?
2. Did you feel you could do things in a different order?
3. Did anything happen later because of something you did earlier?
4. Did you ever change your mind about what to do?
5. Did you feel the game was telling you the answer, or did you feel it was your decision?
6. Which choice felt most important?
7. Was there a moment where you wanted to try another approach?
8. Did anything feel confusing?
9. Did anything feel boring/repetitive?
10. Would you play another 20 minutes?
11. What do you think Manimekalai should do next?

## The two questions that matter most

After everything else, these two answers decide whether 0.3 did its job:

- **"Did you feel you were making choices, or did you feel the game was telling you
  exactly what to do?"**
- **"Would you play another 20 minutes?"**

## Evidence discipline (for whoever writes up results later)

- Qualitative, owner-reported. Not statistical validation.
- Do not invent participant identities, ages, counts, verbatim quotes, or ratings.
- Do not store personally identifiable information about children.
- "The player enjoyed it" is not the bar; "the player felt the outcome was theirs" is.
