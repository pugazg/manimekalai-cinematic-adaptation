# G2 Prototype 0.2 — Playtest Record

Date: 2026-08-11
Prototype: G2 Prototype 0.2 — Human First
Status: Owner-reported qualitative playtest completed

## What was done

The repository owner tested G2 Prototype 0.2 ("Human First") with child players
and asked the planned post-play debrief questions. The owner reports that the
child-player debrief **supported the Prototype 0.2 milestone across all planned
questions.** Exact participant responses are not recorded in the repository. The
planned questions were:

1. Who were you trying to help?
2. Why couldn't Paati get food at first?
3. What did you have to change?
4. How did you feel when Paati finally ate and said "போதும்"?
5. Which person did you care about most?
6. Was there any part where you didn't know what to do?
7. Was anything boring?
8. Did you want to continue playing and find out what happens to Manimekalai next?

## Evidence discipline

This is **qualitative, owner-reported playtest evidence.** It supports preserving
Prototype 0.2 as the current baseline but does **not** establish general usability
or audience-wide validation.

To keep this record honest, it deliberately does **not** invent or assert:

- participant names;
- participant ages;
- the number of participants;
- verbatim quotes;
- numerical ratings or scores;
- behavioural observations the owner did not provide.

No statistical validation is claimed, and **no personally identifiable information
about any child is stored** in this repository.

## Decision

Prototype 0.2 is **accepted as the first preserved playable baseline.** It has now
passed both:

1. **technical validation** — lint clean, 33/33 automated tests, successful build,
   and a completed browser playthrough; and
2. **the current qualitative child-playtest milestone** — the owner-reported debrief
   above, which the owner reports supported the milestone across all planned questions.

Prototype 0.3 **may now be designed** after this prototype is integrated into the
repository and reviewed. This record does not authorise starting Prototype 0.3 work.
