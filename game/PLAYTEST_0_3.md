# G2 Prototype 0.3 — Playtest Record

Date: 2026-08-13
Prototype: G2 Prototype 0.3 — Meaningful Agency
Status: Owner-reported qualitative playtest completed

## What was done

The repository owner playtested G2 Prototype 0.3 ("Meaningful Agency") and reports that
it was **successful and produced the expected results** for the milestone. The milestone
existed to test one central question — *does the player feel they are making meaningful
choices, or merely discovering the developer's intended solution?* — and a secondary one:
*after 20–25 minutes, do they want to keep playing?*

The owner reports that the playtest outcome **supported the Prototype 0.3 milestone
across the planned debrief.** (The debrief is not a yes/no checklist — several questions
are open-ended or negatively phrased — so this is stated as milestone support, not
"positive answers to all questions.") In particular, the owner reports the playtest
supported the intended 0.3 goals around:

- **meaningful player choice** — the outcome reflected how the player chose to help,
  rather than a single developer-authored solution;
- **multiple viable approaches** — different reasonable orders of action worked;
- **remembered consequences** — earlier decisions showed up later;
- **the ability to revise decisions** — the player could change and reassign;
- **sustained interest across the longer prototype** — engagement held over ~20–25 min;
- **retention of clarity and emotional connection** — the validated 0.2 foundation
  (child-readable interaction, the child/Paati spine, the "போதும்" beat) was preserved.

## The planned debrief questions

These are the questions the plan set out (see `PLAYTEST_0_3_PLAN.md`). They are recorded
here for provenance; **no answers are transcribed or invented.**

1. Who did you choose to help first? Why?
2. Did you feel you could do things in a different order?
3. Did anything happen later because of something you did earlier?
4. Did you ever change your mind about what to do?
5. Did you feel the game was telling you the answer, or did you feel it was your decision?
6. Which choice felt most important?
7. Was there a moment where you wanted to try another approach?
8. Did anything feel confusing?
9. Did anything feel boring or repetitive?
10. Would you play another 20 minutes?
11. What do you think Manimekalai should do next?

The two that matter most for this milestone: *"Did you feel you were making choices, or
that the game was telling you exactly what to do?"* and *"Would you play another 20
minutes?"*

## Evidence discipline

This is **qualitative, owner-reported playtest evidence.** It supports preserving
Prototype 0.3 as the current digital-game baseline but does **not** establish broad
usability, statistical validation, commercial viability, or audience-wide preference.

To keep this record honest, it deliberately does **not** invent or assert:

- the number of players;
- player ages;
- participant identities;
- exact answers or quotations;
- numerical ratings or preference percentages;
- completion times that were not actually recorded;
- behavioural observations the owner did not provide.

**No personally identifiable information about any child is stored** in this repository.

## Decision

Prototype 0.3 is **accepted as the current validated playable baseline.** It has now
passed:

1. **technical validation** — lint clean, automated tests green, successful build, and
   a browser playthrough;
2. **the previously validated child clarity / emotional foundation inherited from 0.2**
   — preserved and re-checked (meaning icons, action-specific prompts, child/Paati spine,
   the "போதும்" payoff); and
3. **the current qualitative meaningful-agency and sustained-engagement milestone** —
   the owner-reported outcome above.

Prototype 0.4 **may be designed only after** Prototype 0.3 is integrated into the
repository and reviewed. This record does not authorise starting Prototype 0.4 work, and
does not define its scope.
