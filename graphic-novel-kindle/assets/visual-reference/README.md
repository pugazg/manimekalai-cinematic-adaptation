# Visual Reference Assets

This folder stores selected visual-reference studies for the isolated Kindle graphic-novel workstream.

These assets are **production references, not historical proof and not automatically final publication art**. Generated assets remain `[INTERPRETATION]` and must pass visual, historical, continuity and rights review before they can be promoted to a locked production asset.

## GN-ASSET-0007 — Manimekalai

### v2.0 expression study

File: `GN-ASSET-0007_manimekalai_expression_v2.0.jpg`

Status: **REVIEW STUDY — not yet locked**

Purpose:

- correct the insufficient expression differentiation in the first Manimekalai study;
- test a wider acting range for graphic-novel storytelling;
- establish consistency of face, hair and body language across emotional states;
- keep costume treatment provisional until the costume evidence package is separately reviewed.

Expression targets include:

- observant/listening;
- quiet happiness;
- unease;
- shock;
- fear;
- grief;
- anger/moral outrage;
- defiance/refusal;
- compassion;
- confusion/questioning;
- exhaustion;
- resolve.

The image itself must not be treated as proof of Manimekalai's exact age, clothing, jewellery, hairstyle or historical appearance. Those remain subject to the parent repository's evidence and caution rules.

## Storage policy

Keep lightweight review/reference exports here when they are useful for continuity and auditability. Large layered masters and bulk page-art working files should remain outside Git until the binary-asset strategy is deliberately locked.
