## Major editorial removals

### Removed: guarded cistern / official order sequence

The v0.2 Page 6 sequence showed:

- guarded water storage;
- an official refusing action without an order;
- an off-panel authority commanding access;
- a caption equating good government with opening a gate.

This was acknowledged as downstream screenplay invention rather than Chapter 1 text. It consumed one eighth of the pilot while several actual canto features were missing.

**Decision:** remove from Chapter 1.

The idea remains available elsewhere in the cinematic adaptation, but this graphic novel follows a stricter primary-first hierarchy.

### Removed/reduced: contemporary thesis dialogue

The v0.2 grain-seller line — “If the festival belongs to everyone... let the measure be fair to everyone too” — was conceptually valid but too explicit and modern in rhetorical shape.

**Decision:** replace argumentative dialogue with object-led captions and the level grain measure. The social point is now made through juxtaposition before assertion.

### Removed: Madhavi's invented reply

The v0.2 Page 8 line — “Let it begin for the city” — gave Madhavi a polished statement before Chapter 2 had earned her speaking position.

**Decision:** Madhavi remains silent. The silence itself becomes the handoff into the city's gossip.
