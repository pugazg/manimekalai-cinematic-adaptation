# Source and Rights Policy

## Purpose

This document governs the Kindle graphic-novel workstream only. It does not alter the repository's existing rights position or any screenplay/source register.

## Source hierarchy

### Tier 1 — classical epic

The classical Tamil *Manimekalai* is the primary narrative authority for plot, named characters, core events, philosophical journey and chapter architecture.

Rules:

- verify exact Tamil quotations against the selected primary edition before publication;
- do not import wording from a modern translation merely because the underlying epic is old;
- record editorial reconstruction when a scene combines material from more than one classical passage.

### Tier 2 — repository research

Existing historical-world, character, philosophy, production-design and evidence work may inform the graphic novel.

This material is a research dependency, not a licence to claim certainty. Existing labels remain meaningful:

- `[TEXT]`
- `[CROSS]`
- `[HISTORY]`
- `[INTERPRETATION]`
- `[CAUTION]`

Every finished page is still a visual interpretation.

### Tier 3 — Bharathidasan, *Manimekalai Venpa*

Perspective tag:

`PERSPECTIVE: VENPA-BHARATHIDASAN`

Default use type:

`THEMATIC`

The workstream may draw on registered emphases such as:

- hunger as a central social antagonist;
- renunciation as active public responsibility;
- women's choice and autonomy;
- compassion preceding miraculous efficacy;
- Udayakumaran as tragic through refusal to accept another person's choice;
- Aputhiran as an ethical and philosophical centre;
- household food-giving as skilled social action.

A graphic-novel borrowing that goes beyond broad theme should receive a local record:

`GN-VENPA-USE-### | chapter/page | element | use type | verification | rights status`

Use types:

- `DIRECT`
- `ADAPTED`
- `COMBINED`
- `THEMATIC`
- `REJECTED`
- `DEFERRED`

### Tier 4 — cinematic screenplay

The existing screenplay may be consulted for staging, dialogue economy and scene compression. It is downstream adaptation material and must not be mistaken for primary evidence.

## Bharathidasan flavour policy

The desired flavour should emerge from **ethical emphasis, rhythm, imagery and social directness**, not from copying identifiable lines.

Allowed before verse-level verification and rights clearance:

- short, forceful caption rhythm;
- lyrical contrast between splendour and hunger;
- emphasis on dignity of ordinary labour;
- compassion framed as social action;
- women speaking their own decisions;
- philosophical ideas tested in material life;
- newly written Tamil metaphors that do not reproduce Bharathidasan wording.

Blocked until cleared:

- verbatim or near-verbatim Bharathidasan verses;
- reconstructed lines based only on OCR memory;
- scanned pages or third-party editions reproduced as images;
- modern translations without explicit reuse permission;
- third-party illustrations, photographs, maps or costume plates without publication rights.

## Artwork provenance

Every final or candidate image should have a sidecar record containing:

- asset ID;
- chapter/page/panel IDs;
- creator or generation tool;
- generation/edit date;
- final prompt or commission brief;
- reference assets used;
- whether those references are owned, public-domain, licensed or internal-only;
- post-processing performed;
- commercial-use status;
- human review status;
- source/evidence labels;
- known historical uncertainty.

No image becomes final merely because it looks convincing.

## AI image policy

Generated art may be used only if the production workflow and publishing terms applicable at final release permit commercial use.

The book must not present generated reconstructions as archaeologically exact. Character design should avoid copying a recognisable living actor or private individual unless the necessary rights and consent exist.

## Fonts

Tamil lettering fonts must have a licence that permits embedding/rasterisation and commercial eBook distribution. Font files themselves must not be committed unless redistribution is explicitly allowed.

## Territory gate

KDP publication rights are selected by territory. Before publication, the project must complete a territory-by-territory rights check for:

- the exact classical edition used for quotations;
- Bharathidasan material actually quoted or closely adapted;
- translations;
- fonts;
- commissioned or generated artwork;
- cover assets;
- any scholarly excerpt beyond minimal quotation.

Until that review is complete, the project should prefer original narration and dialogue based on public-domain narrative facts and independently written interpretation.
