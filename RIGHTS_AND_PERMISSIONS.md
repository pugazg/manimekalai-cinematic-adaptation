# Rights and Permissions

This repository separates original project research from source texts and third-party materials.

## Interim owner decision

**Option B — fully reserved interim state — selected 2026-08-03.**

All rights are reserved in original project material to the extent owned by
the repository owner or identified contributors. No licence to reproduce,
adapt, translate, redistribute, perform, produce, publish, sublicense or
commercially exploit that original material is granted merely because the
repository is publicly viewable.

This interim decision does not claim rights in the classical epic,
third-party translations, Bharathidasan material, scholarship, quotations,
images or other content owned by others. Those materials remain subject to
their underlying rights and applicable law.

See the root `NOTICE` and the rights notices in `docs/`, screenplay,
storyboard, release and tooling directories. Qualified `SR-013` review
remains open. Reconsider this interim state by 2027-02-03, or earlier if
legal review, financing or collaboration requires it.

## Do not commit by default

- full translations still under copyright;
- scans or PDFs without clear redistribution permission;
- photographs, maps, artwork or articles copied from third parties;
- proprietary production references.

Store lawfully held research copies under `private_sources/`, which is ignored by Git.

## Permitted repository content

- original research notes;
- short, properly attributed quotations within applicable legal limits;
- bibliographic metadata;
- public-domain source text where status is documented;
- original adaptation decisions, screenplay drafts and production documents.

Every source record should include rights status as one of:

- `public_domain`
- `open_licence`
- `permission_obtained`
- `citation_only`
- `status_unknown`

Unknown status means the material must not be redistributed.
